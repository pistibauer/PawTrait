Privacy Policy

PawTrait by iBaud

Last updated: 29 October 2026

The short version. PawTrait paints your dog's portrait entirely on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded. We have no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and we do not collect, sell or share any information about you.

Who we are

PawTrait ("the app") is developed by iBaud ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Photos you choose

You pick a photo with Apple's system photo picker. The app only receives the photos you select; it cannot see the rest of your library. The photo is processed entirely on your device using Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks to find your dog, crop the head and paint the portrait. Photos are not uploaded, stored on any server, or shared with anyone.

Saving portraits

When you tap Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add the finished portrait to your photo library. This permission is add-only: the app cannot read, change or delete anything in your library. When you tap Share, the portrait goes only where you choose to send it, using the standard iOS share sheet.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No uploading of your photos or portraits.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No selling of personal information.
  • No location collection.

Children

PawTrait is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your control over your data

The app does not keep a copy of your photos or portraits after you close it, apart from what you choose to save to Photos or share. Photo library permission can be changed at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and publish the revised version here.
