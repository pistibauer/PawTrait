Getting the best portrait

  • Use a photo where your dog's face is clear, well lit and in focus.
  • Leave a little space around the head, so both ears are fully in the photo.
  • On the Frame the head screen, drag and pinch until the head and both ears sit inside the circle.
  • Tap a style or splash color to repaint. Each repaint gives a fresh splash arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

The app didn't find my dog. What can I do?

Try a photo where the dog stands out clearly from the background, then adjust the circle with Adjust head crop.

An ear was cut off.

PawTrait always keeps the ears inside the portrait, but it can't restore an ear that runs off the edge of the original photo. Choose a photo with some space around the head.

How do I save or print my portrait?

Tap Save to Photos for a full-resolution copy, or Share to send it to a printing service, messages or social apps.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Portraits are painted entirely on your iPhone. See our privacy policy.
